radio_button_checkedThe Dynamic Island

One pill that tells you what just happened.

Music, notifications, volume, timers, downloads, recording, Caps Lock, new devices. It grows only as much as the moment needs, then settles back into a clock.

  • touch_app
    Hover to peek. Now playing with artwork glow, or what you're working on: git branch, changes, CPU and GPU.
  • swipe
    Scroll for volume, Shift+scroll for brightness, middle-click to play or pause, right-click for background apps.
  • sync
    Long jobs report progress. lumen run -- make puts a live bar in the island and tells you when it's done.
tuneControl centre

The icon is the switch. The tile opens the list.

One control never does two things. Wi-Fi groups what you're on, what you've saved and what's nearby. Bluetooth finds nearby devices only while you're looking, and pairs them in one click.

  • wifi
    Wi-Fi: Connected · Saved · Other networks, a password field in place, and ⋯ for Disconnect or Forget. Ethernet is detected too.
  • bluetooth_searching
    Bluetooth: your devices with battery levels, then Nearby. Click to pair; it's trusted, so it reconnects by itself.
  • grid_view
    Sixteen toggles, per-app volume, now playing, live system stats and a calendar. Arrow keys work everywhere.

mouse Try it: click a tile to open its list, click the round icon to switch it.

usbDevice alerts

Plug something in. The island knows what it is.

USB devices are recognised by type. The first time you connect a device you get a card with a “New” badge. Drives get Open and Eject, displays get Arrange. Devices you use every day get a quiet one-line note when they connect or disconnect, with their real name.

view_sidebarNotifications & planner

Your messages on the right, your day on the left.

Notifications group by app and swipe away. The planner holds weather, an agenda you write in plain language (“fri 7pm Concert”), to-dos and a notes pad that saves as you type.

searchOverview

Tap Super. Type what you mean.

Apps and windows, maths, settings, commands with parameters, timers, projects and your clipboard, ranked into groups as you type.

  • terminal
    Commands that take values: volume 40, brightness 70, open project lumen, kill slack.
  • bookmark
    Snapshots: save coding and restore coding bring back a whole layout.
  • drag_pan
    Live workspace previews. Drag a window onto another workspace to move it.

content_pasteClipboard & switching

A clipboard that knows what you copied.

Links, emails, colours, paths, commands and code are recognised and filterable. Anything that looks like a secret is masked. Enter pastes straight into the app you were in.

  • keyboard
    Enter pastes, Ctrl+Enter pastes plain, Shift+Enter opens a link, Alt+P pins.
  • tab
    Alt+Tab shows live previews in the order you last used them. Alt+` cycles one app's windows.

    lockLock screen & security

    A glance to unlock. Your password always works.

    A big clock, now playing, battery and notification counts (never their content). Face ID through Gaze unlocks only the lock screen, never sudo or your keyring. Admin prompts are drawn by Lumen and say exactly what's asking.

    • face
      Face ID with liveness checks; on battery the camera starts only when you press F2.
    • admin_panel_settings
      Polkit agent built in. A clear card, the app's name, and the exact action.
    • shield
      Security page: firewall, SSH, Secure Boot, disk encryption and SELinux at a glance.

    settingsSettings

    Twenty pages, grouped, searchable, and live.

    Changes apply the moment you make them: to the shell, windows, terminal and lock screen. The Advanced page shows the config files, checks them, opens the logs and reloads everything.

    appsYour apps, in Lumen colours

    Dolphin, Kate, Okular and friends match the desktop.

    Lumen generates a KDE colour scheme, the Darkly style and your icon theme for Qt apps, so they look like part of the desktop. It applies only inside Lumen: Plasma and your own settings are left untouched.

    downloadInstall

    A guided install that asks before every step.

    Packages come from your distribution's own repositories: Fedora, Arch, Debian/Ubuntu and openSUSE. Fonts and the cursor are optional downloads from their official projects. Nothing is ever piped into a shell, and ./uninstall.sh removes it again.

    codeGet it on GitHub menu_bookInstall guide
    ❯ git clone https://github.com/Anasarfeen123/lumen.git ~/Projects/lumen
    ❯ cd ~/Projects/lumen
    ❯ ./install.sh --dry-run # shows every step; changes nothing
    ❯ ./install.sh # asks before each step

    Just curious? bin/lumen-session runs Lumen in a window inside your current Wayland desktop.
